Privacy policy

Stelline collects no data

Effective date: 3 September 2026. Applies to the Stelline app for iPhone and to this website.

In one paragraph: Stelline does not collect, store, transmit, sell or share any personal data — or any data at all — about you. The app has no internet access, no account or sign-in, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party software inside it. Your settings and your saved game exist only on your iPhone and are deleted when you delete the app. There is nothing for us to access, correct or erase, because we never receive anything.

1. Who this policy covers

“Stelline”, “the app”, “we” and “us” refer to the Stelline puzzle game for iPhone and to its independent developer, who publishes it on the App Store. “You” means anyone who installs or uses the app, or visits this website. This policy is written to be jurisdiction-neutral; sections 8 and 9 address the questions that European (GDPR and UK GDPR), Californian (CCPA/CPRA) and similar privacy laws ask.

2. Data we collect: none

The app does not collect any information from you or about you, and does not send anything off your device. In particular it does not collect or process:

  • your name, email address, phone number or any account details (there is no account);
  • identifiers such as a device ID, advertising identifier or user ID;
  • your location, contacts, photos, calendar, health data or any other data on your iPhone;
  • usage data, gameplay statistics, session recordings or telemetry;
  • crash reports or diagnostics generated by the app itself;
  • purchase or payment information (the app has no in-app purchases);
  • anything typed, tapped or drawn in the app.

The app does not use tracking, fingerprinting, cookies or any similar technology. It never asks for a permission from iOS, because it needs none. Its App Store privacy label is Data Not Collected, and its privacy manifest declares no collected data types, no tracking and no tracking domains.

3. No network access

Stelline contains no networking code. It does not connect to the internet, to our servers (we have none) or to anyone else's, whether Wi-Fi or cellular is available or not. Puzzles are generated and checked entirely on your iPhone. Everything the app does works exactly the same in Airplane Mode.

4. What stays on your iPhone

To work as a game, the app keeps a small amount of information locally, inside its own private storage area (the app “sandbox”) on your iPhone. None of it identifies you, and none of it ever leaves the device through the app.

Local data kept by the app
WhatWhereWhy
Your settings: the mode, board size and difficulty you last chose, Auto-check and auto-dot toggles, timer visibility, piece and mark glyphs, theme, colour-blind mode, appearance and haptics; and a random number used to seed the puzzle generator. The app's user defaults (a standard iOS preferences store private to the app). So the app opens the way you left it.
The game in progress: the board, your marks, the time elapsed, the hints used and the undo and redo history. A file in the app's private Application Support folder. So Continue can resume your game after the app is closed.
A small bank of puzzles generated in advance. A file in the same private folder. So New puzzle is instant.

Deleting the app deletes all of it. iOS removes an app's private storage, including its user defaults and files, when the app is uninstalled. There is no copy anywhere else that we could keep or restore.

Device backups. Like the data of any app, these files may be included in the backups you make of your iPhone (iCloud Backup or a backup to a computer), according to your own iOS settings. Backups are made by Apple's software under your Apple Account and are governed by Apple's privacy policy; the app has no part in them and we cannot access them.

5. Third parties: none

The app contains no third-party software development kits, libraries or services: no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, no social features, no cloud sync. It is built only with Apple's system frameworks and our own code. Consequently, no third party receives data from the app, and we do not sell, share, rent or disclose personal information to anyone, because we have none.

6. Apple, the App Store and TestFlight

You download Stelline from Apple's App Store, or from TestFlight when testing a pre-release version. Apple processes the data involved in downloading, updating and (for TestFlight) testing apps under Apple's privacy policy, independently of us.

If you have chosen to share analytics with app developers in iOS (Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements → Share With App Developers), Apple may make aggregated, anonymised crash logs and usage statistics for the app available to us through Apple's developer tools. That data is collected by Apple, not by the app, contains no information that identifies you, and stays within Apple's systems; you can turn the sharing off at any time in the same setting. TestFlight testers can likewise choose whether TestFlight shares crash logs and feedback with the developer.

7. Children

Stelline is suitable for all ages and is rated 4+ on the App Store. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children under 13 (or the age of digital consent that applies where you live), and contains no ads, no purchases and no chat. The only web addresses it can open are the pages of this site (support and this policy) and the contact form linked from Settings, hosted by Tally, and only when you tap them; they open in your browser, outside the app.

8. Your rights under GDPR, CCPA and similar laws

Privacy laws such as the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulations, the California Consumer Privacy Act and its equivalents in other places give you rights over personal data an organisation holds about you: to access it, correct it, delete it, receive a copy of it, restrict or object to its use, opt out of its sale or sharing, and not to be discriminated against for exercising those rights.

Because Stelline does not process any personal data, we hold nothing to which these rights could apply. We are not a controller or processor of your personal data in relation to the app, we have no legal basis to cite because there is no processing, we retain nothing, we transfer nothing across borders, we do not sell or share personal information, and we make no automated decisions about you. The only data that exists is the local game data described in section 4, which is under your sole control: to erase it, delete the app.

If you nonetheless believe we hold personal data about you, for example because you emailed us, you can ask us to access, correct or delete it using the contact below, and we will do so. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

9. This website

This website is a set of static pages. It sets no cookies, uses no analytics and loads no fonts, scripts, images or other resources from third parties, with one exception: the contact form on the support page, described below. It is published through a static hosting provider (currently GitHub Pages, a service of GitHub, Inc.), which, like any web host, may record technical information such as the IP address of visitors in its server logs to deliver the pages and protect its service; that processing is described in GitHub's privacy statement. We do not receive those logs.

Support email. If you write to support@stelline.page, we receive your address and your message in iCloud Mail (a service of Apple Inc.), use them only to answer you, and delete the exchange within 24 months of the last message. The app never attaches anything to an email on its own; you decide what to send.

Contact form. The form on the support page is provided by Tally BV (Gent, Belgium), which stores what you submit on servers in the European Union and acts as our processor under its data processing agreement. Each submission reaches us by email at support@stelline.page; we use it only to answer you and to improve Stelline, and delete submissions from Tally within 12 months. The form uses Google reCAPTCHA to block spam, which loads a script from Google when the form is shown and is covered by Google's privacy policy. Every field except the message is optional, and nothing from the app is attached on its own.

Feedback board. The public ideas and roadmap board at stelline.featurebase.app is provided by Featurebase (Cordnet OÜ, Tallinn, Estonia) and hosted in the European Union. What you post or vote on there is public; you can take part as a guest without creating an account, and anything you enter (a name or an email, if you choose to give one) is handled under Featurebase's privacy policy. We read the board to plan the game and do not copy your details anywhere else.

If you email us, we receive what you send (your address and your message) and use it only to reply. We do not add you to any list.

10. Changes to this policy

If the app ever changes in a way that affects your privacy — for example if a future version added an optional online feature — we would update this policy first, change the effective date at the top, describe the change plainly, and update the app's App Store privacy label to match. The current version of this policy is always the one published at this address.

11. Contact

Questions about privacy, or about anything else: email support@stelline.page or use the contact form on the support page.
